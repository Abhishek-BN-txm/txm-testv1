No public year-by-year ramp exists for the 4.5 GW. APS says the 2026 IRP (due 2026-10-30) will be "the first line of sight" on the ramp. Context (baseline S11): XHLF share of sales goes from 15% (2026) to 40% (2041), and the 2041 peak is 11,700 MW.

Retail-sales guidance (baseline S7): data centers and large manufacturing add 3-5% in 2026 and 4-6%/yr through 2030.

APS's 2025 data-center share of peak was "about 5%" (APS data-center page). A typical data center in APS territory is "approximately 400 MW", with requests of up to 2,000 MW.

## Tariffs and conditions
- XHLF rate: for loads of at least 5,000 kW with a load factor of at least 92% in 9 of the prior 12 months; 15,000 kW for the sustainability option.
- The rate case (2025 filing; hearing 2026-05-18 to 07-07) proposes a more than 45% increase for XHLF customers, plus a Formula Rate Adjustment Mechanism. The ALJ's ROO is expected in late November 2026, the ACC vote by 2026-12-31 and new rates in early 2027 (ACC 2026-07-10).
- APS describes minimum bills, collateral, and long-term contracts with up-front contributions (APS data-center FAQ).
- Subscription security of $750M per 100 MW: secondary only (Atrium, citing testimony). Verify in docket E-01345A-25-0105. The docket number itself also comes only from a secondary source (a Facebook post); confirm it.
- Customer-funded transmission: TSMC funded the Biscuit Flats relocation. Baccara's developer pays all costs (ACC).

## Conflicts between sources
1. **2026 peak.** APS says 9,164 MW on 2026-08-02 (Q2 call, RTO Insider). EIA-930 AZPS shows 8,982 MW (baseline). The 2025 record is 8,648 MW in RTO Insider but 8,631 MW in the RPAC deck (S11).
2. **ACC workshop summary.** It says APS "estimates its peak demand load for large customers will reach approximately 13.1 GW this year". That is inconsistent with every APS figure (system peak about 9.2 GW) and is likely a misstatement of a queue or total figure. Check APS's own workshop filing (eDocket item 462341).
3. **Committed load.** 4.5 GW of XHLF (calls) against "about 4,000 MW" for data centers (KJZZ). These are probably consistent: data centers are a subset.
4. **Uncommitted queue.** Reported as 18, 19 and ~20 GW at different dates.
5. **Cotton corridor / Jojoba-Rudd timing.** 2030-2031 in the TYP; "2031/2032" on the APS project page; 2032 on the STX page.
6. **Runway/Diamond CEC.** APS page says the LSC granted CEC-209, then cites the ACC approval of "CEC-200" on 2023-01-10. The TYP says CEC 209, so "CEC-200" is likely a typo.
7. **Project Baccara site.** ACC: "NE corner Olive Ave & Bullard Ave alignment". ABC15: "off Olive Ave and Litchfield Rd".
8. **Stream PHXA substation.** 350 MW in Stream's earlier release; 480 MW on the current page.
9. **TSMC MW.** About 200 MW for Phase 1 (2022). ">1 GW" prior commitment (2026). ">1,200 MW for Facilities 1-6 by 2033" (Anthem, secondary).
10. **Vantage AZ1.** 176 MW (Vantage) vs 164 MW (Southland).
