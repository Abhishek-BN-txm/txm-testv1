# APS procured generation and storage, Oct 2023 to Oct 2026

Companion to `aps_procured_resources.csv`, which has one row per resource with sources and queue-match reasoning. Figures are quoted as the sources state them. Pinnacle West's FY2025 10-K is the controlling list of PPAs and owned projects.

## RFP context
- **2022 ASRFP:** "approximately 1,000 to 1,500 MW", in service 2025 to June 2027.
- **2023 ASRFP:** ~1 GW sought, "at least 700 MW of renewable resources", in service 2026 to 2028. The result, announced 2024-11-20, was "nearly 7,300 MW" of nameplate capacity, 93% of it clean. APS called this its "largest-ever" energy supply. Nearly every resource below comes from this RFP.
- **2024 ASRFP:** "approximately 2,000 MW", targeting a 2028 COD. Proposals were due 2025-02-05, and APS preferred projects of 600 MW or less. No awards are public as of Oct 2026.
- **2025 ASRFP:** "at least 1,000 MWs", for CODs from 2029-06-01 to 2031-06-01. It includes the Desert Sun opportunity. On the Q2-2026 call, APS said "final awards expected later this year."

Storage MWh stated by developers, where known: Maricopa EC 2,200; Hashknife 1,900 (both phases); Snowflake A 1,900; Pioneer 1,200; Catclaw 1,000; Beehive 1,000; Justice 600; Desert Bloom 600; Kitt 400; White Tank 400.

**Planned and not yet approved:** Desert Sun, a 2,000 MW APS-owned gas plant near Gila Bend, announced 2025-10-30. Phase 1 is due in late 2030. Phase 2 would be funded through a large-customer subscription model; APS sized the initial offering at "1 to 1.2 gigawatts."

**Approvals:** The Redhawk expansion received its CEC on 2024-08-22, and the ACC approved it on 2024-10-08. West Camp's gen-tie CEC dates from 2022-10-18. Contracts were announced as follows:
- 2024: Justice (Jul 15), Papago Solar and Desert Bloom (Oct 31), Beehive (Nov 4), Snowflake A (Nov 8), White Tank (Nov 12), Kitt (Dec 9).
- 2025: Maricopa EC (Jan 17), Pioneer (Feb 4).

**Mix:** The slate is about 93% clean. Gas is about 7% of the 2023 slate, but the pipeline is shifting toward gas. Desert Sun accounts for 2,000 MW, and the 2026 queue entries include several new gas requests of 500 to 1,266 MW each.

## Caveats
- The standalone BESS matches in the Phoenix Metro and Gila Bend areas are weak. No source names a POI, so treat those matches as hypotheses.
- Hashknife's split across Q230, Q270 and Q271 is inferred from the shared Cholla 500 kV gen-tie and from MW.
- Several matched requests show an "In-Service" or "In Construction" status. That fits 2025–2026 CODs.
- The 10-K's MW figures sometimes differ from the developers' (for example, Snowflake A is 475 MW in the 10-K versus "600 MW" (MWdc) from the developer, and Catclaw's solar is 225 versus 250 MWac). The CSV keeps both figures.
